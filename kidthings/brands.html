ABOUT KID THINGS

Kid Things aims to make the lives of modern parents just a little easier by providing you a free stylist text service to help you search for the perfect kid's outfits. We help your little ones stay warm, comfortable and cute! We stay within your budget, and free up your time. The best part? For every $100 dollars your spend, we contribute one hour's worth of early childhood education to disadvantaged children.

Anica John created Kid Things in 2016. She's been working with family technology and consumer companies for several years. Anica is passionate about building a new model of philanthropy - one that allows consumers to give to important causes with every purchase. You can read more about that here. Anica lives with her husband and baby in San Francisco, CA.
