Art Golf Classics and Craig Blair

Craig Blair, president and driving force of Art Golf Classics Inc. is a lover of all things golf. He has been a fan of the game, a golfer, and a collector of golf antiques, books and memorabilia for over 30 years.

His interest in the game and all it's facets has given him many years of pleasure and a certain expertise, and an extensive pool of knowledge to draw upon. He is a member of The Golf Collectors Society, an international organization dedicated to preserving the treasures and traditions of the game of golf.

He has a wonderful collection of books by some of the world's best golf historians and authors and has probably read them each several times!
