Double Ribbon Mixer

Double Ribbom Screw Mixer with full width discharge bottom provide very fast discharge of mixer and are suitable for plants having mixer fitted at some height (i.e., on struchure) and have surge bin below it. Due to fast discharging, segregation at discharge is greatly avoided.


Construction :

These mixers consists of U-shaped horizontal trough in which a heavy duty shaft with two ribbons in helix shape at different pitch circle diameter are mounted with the help of arms. The ribbon helix is so shaped that when inside mixing shaft rotates, one ribbon pushes the material in one side while at same time other ribbon pushes the material opposite side. So, particles inside the mixer are given very fast movement and homogenous mix is achieved.

Feature

Salient Features :

Fitted with air-exchange pipe for smooth filling and discharge of mixer. Assessing doors interlocked with main motor, when opened will not let the mixer to start.

Drive System :

Suitable combination Inline helical gear box, shaft mounted gear box with V-Belts , or chain sprocket as per suitability to system requirement is used to ensure that it is highly reliable with good factor safety, energy efficient and maintenance free.

Air-Exchange Pipe :

Based on Internationally acknowledged testing methods, this test is performed on mixers for validating mixing efficiency. In this test 25 gm of tracer is added in one ton of feed for one ton mixer, and given specific mixing time. After this samples (casually ten) from different location in mixer are taken and availability of no. of tracer particles in equal weight of 100 gm of feed sample is determined.


Our Patented Desgin:

Double ribbon screw mixer is being manufactured in this world from the last many decsdes with common design features ofn inner and outer blades.one or more of following problems are generally faced in these design :-

  • 1. Bending of ribbon and arms particularly near to side walls

  • 2. Excessive product heating.

  • 3. Longer mixing time.

  • 4. More power per ton of mixing.

  • 5. Mixing not up to the mark.

For achieving same mixing result on any other mixer in tits class it will at leat save 25-35 percent of time,similarly at least 25-35 percent of power is saved

In our patented desgin, both outer and inner ribbons are having variables cross section area that is wider at the start of the ribbon and narrower at the end, both ribbons are having end relief. With these modifications , we have achived following results:

  • 1. Breakage bending of ribbons completely eliminated.
  • 2. very less heat is generated.
  • 3. Mixing efficency:Due to the usage of much wider blades,more pitch of blades,higher rpm,the mixing efficency of these mixers is the highest in its class world over.
