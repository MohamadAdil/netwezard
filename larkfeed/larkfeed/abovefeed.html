Here we are giving brief details of our Standard Mash Feed Plants upto 16 ton/hr.


DESIRED FEATURES OF AN IDEAL MASH FEED PLANT

  • 1. It should give consistent output for which it is designed.

  • 2. In Grinding it should give desired texture with uniform grinding.

  • 3. In mixing it should give accurate mixing in shortest time.

  • 4. It should have provision of reliable magnetic separator.

  • 5. Ease of control in feeding of raw materials.

  • 6. All the machines should be of matching capacity.

  • 7. It should have minimum dust with good working conditions.

  • 8. It should consume minimum energy per ton of feed processed.

  • 9. Highly reliable with minimum breakdown.

  • 10. Need minimum maintenance.

  • 11. Should have safety features and dust free peration
