Half Screen Hammer Mill

This machine is used for reducing the particle size of different ingredients in feed .

It consists of a rotor on which swinging hammers are mounted in different patterns which when rotate at high speed, strike with incoming feed ingredients and break these.

Required fineness of final product is obtained by changing the screens of different hole size in grinder..
