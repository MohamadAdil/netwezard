CLINICAL TRIALS

Same number of calories but 2.5X more weight loss and 3.2X more inches with DASH diet among diabetics

A clinical trial published in the ADA journals examined the effect of the DASH diet on cardiovascular risks among 31 diabetics with metabolic syndrome. All participants received a 500 Kcal daily deficit diet, however, the DASH group lost 11 Lbs. vs 4.4 Lbs. and 2.64" vs 0.75" as well as improvement in FBL mg/dl and A1C% (-29.4 vs 12.4 and -1.7 vs -0.5 respectively) compared to the control diet.
