  • Examination Fees Free
  • Visa Filing
  • Visa training
  • Airline Ticketing
  • Airport Pickup from Germany
  • 1 year Accomadtion as a part of Scholorship Program
  • 100€ Pocket money/Month
  • City Tour
  • 1 year Insurance and Blocked Account
  • Eurotrip
  • Student Panel ( 24*7)
  • Two years of Extended service Contract with the student in Germany
  • Permenant Residency/German Passport Assistance

ABSOLUTLY FREE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM

- In 2020 only Cameroun and Indian citizens can apply for scholarship exam
- There is No age limit for appearing in the scholarship examination

Scholarship Program for the students in Africa

  • Student currently studying in class 13th and who completed 13 years are eligible for Scholarship from KKV Foundation
  • A Nationwide Scholarship Examination is conducted to determine the student’s competency level
  • The Students applying for bachelor’s Scholarship must complete the 13 years of School Education by 2020
  • The Students applying for master’s Scholarship must complete 3 years or 4 years degree program by 2020
  • The students can approach the Schools and Universities of their respective Countries or can enroll for the exam directly via website
ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Scholarship Program for the students in Asia

  • After completion of 12 years of School Education the student is eligible for Scholarship from KKV Foundation
  • A Nationwide Scholarship Examination is conducted to determine the student’s competency level
  • The Students applying for bachelor’s Scholarship must complete the 12 years of School Education by 2020
  • The Students applying for master’s Scholarship must complete 3 years or 4 years degree program by 2020
  • The students can approach the Schools and Universities of their respective Countries or can enroll for the exam directly via website
