Terms &Condtions


Copyright

All photographs, videos on the website, socia media and youtube are copyright of Via Kasi Photography unless otherwise stipulated.


Service

Via Kasi Photography agrees to furnish the services of a professional photographer on the agreed upon date. Via Kasi Photography will be the sole professional photographer employed on this date unless there is someone else who would cover videography or vice versa. In that case, it is the client responsibilitity to ensure that both service providers work together as to ensure that the quality of the work is not compromised. This being said no other photography will be allowed during family portraits and the couple shoot. This is to ensure that Via Kasi Photography can perform their service as professional photographers to the best of their ability. In no circumstances will Via Kasi Photography accept photographs from other photographers to be added to their collection.


Communication and meetings

All communication with Via Kasi Photography is to be done in writing via email for both us and the client records. Via Kasi Photography will meet with clients once, for maximum one hour, at no additional cost unless the client feels no need to meet. This meeting can take place before or after the deposit has been paid.


Payment

Payment of the full deposit is required to secure any booking and booking date. Only once this deposit has been paid will your booking date be secured, until this point, the booking date is held only tentatively for a period of two weeks. Full payment is required one month prior to your event date. In the event of non payment, Via Kasi Photography will retain any payment already made without obligation to perform photographic duties at the event


Payment Schedule

We accept 50% Non Refundable Deposit to confirm the booking of the event date. Then, 30% Payment two months before the event date. Finally, 20% Payment should be made a month before the wedding/event date.


Photos and permissions

Clients will receive their high resolution, edited photos as agreed upon in the package the client has selected. Raw photos will not be provided unless otherwise agreed upon in writing. The client is able to do with these images as they please, but to note, that all images are copyright to Via Kasi Photography and permission is given to Via Kasi Photography to use reproductions of any of these images for display/commercial or editorials, including any online sites (social media and youtube) and magazines. Via Kasi Photography reserves the right to blog any event images. Images are welcome to be shared on social media with credit given to Via Kasi Photography. However, we do ask that clients do not edit or add colour filters to the original images. Clients are welcome to crop images.


Videos

Clients will receive their high resolution, edited video as agreed upon in the package the client has selected. Raw footage will not be provided unless otherwise agreed upon in writing. Once the video has been delivered, editing is completed. Should the client have specific requests for additional editing to the video, it will be evaluated on a case by case basis.


Weddings

For wedding photography, a meal & suitable seating will need to be provided for our photographic team.
Via Kasi Photography recommends that in the case of weddings, the client have a family photographic list to point out important individuals for informal or candid photographs, and to ensure that all family portraits are captured. Via Kasi Photography will not be held responsible for gathering family members for family portraits or for family photos that are not taken.
When booking an engagement session, it is the client’s responsibility to set up a date for the engagement session with Via Kasi Photography. We recommend at least 8 weeks before the wedding date.


Exceeding Booked Duration

Should the event exceed the allocated booked time, the client will be liable for the cost of the extra period. This is charged per hour and Via Kasi Photography shall advise of the cost.


Upgrades and Downgrades

Once a package is booked, with the deposit paid, your package cannot be downgraded to a package of lower value. Upgrades can be made.


Editing

Via Kasi Photography will edit all photos and videos to keep within our artistic & professional standards. In keeping with this,
Our editing does include skin retouching, and other enhancing techniques, but not taking away from authentic beauty.


Photobooks/Storybooks

Photobooks are limited to the number of pages invoiced for. To give an indication, 28 pages can fit a maximum of 75 photos, and 40 pages and can fit a maximum of 125 photos. Additional pages can be requested and will be invoiced for accordingly.
Clients will first receive their photos electronically as agreed upon. Clients are then required to select the photos they would like in the photobook and to send that list to Via Kasi Photography. Via Kasi Photography will then lay the photos out in the photobook after which the client will have the viewing of the photobook proof electronically. Changes are limited to 5 changes, after which R50 per change will be charged. The client has 5 days to make changes after the proofs have been sent, if no feedback is received in that time, the photobook will be assumed approved & sent.


Canvas prints

In the case of canvas prints, the client will first receive their photos electronically as agreed upon. Clients will then select which photo they would like printed.


Delivery

All quoted prices include delivery fees unless the final materials need to be sent internationally in which case the courier fees will be charged to the client at the rate set by the courier.
Via Kasi Photography will aim towards providing the photos no later than 30 – 40 days from your event date.


Cancellation

Should the need arise to cancel a photographic booking for whatever reason, the booking deposit, 50% of the invoice, is non-refundable or exchangeable for another photographic option. If the event date is rescheduled, a new quote will need to be approved & booked. If Via Kasi Photography is not available for the new date requested, the deposit from the original booking is non-refundable & will be retained. Should the event be cancelled or rescheduled within one month before the event, Via Kasi Photography will retain the full payment, deposit & final payment made to date.
All bookings made with Via Kasi Photography automatically agree to these Terms and Conditions, and will be bound once the deposit has been made. In the case of clients not signing and returning the contract, but simply paying the deposit, the client is automatically agreeing to, and understanding all the Terms and Conditions as stipulated in this contract.
Via Kasi Photography reserves the right to change these Terms and Conditions at any point without prior notice. In the event that changes are made and they will be posted to www.viakasiphotography.co.za
