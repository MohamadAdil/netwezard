About Us


Our organization Mesmerizing Himachal Tours and Travels is an inbound tour operator with head office in Shimla, Himachal Pradesh. We aim to offer a range of best value products and services along with good team of professionals who consider even the smallest details of your tour to make your holiday a hassle free and memorable one. It has been prime motto of the organization to earn revenue only if customer is satisfied. Some of the most attractive destinations like Himachal, Srinagar, Uttarakhand, Leh – Ladakh, North, Rajasthan and Kerala are our strong holds.

At Mesmerizing Himachal Tours & Travels, it is our motto to enchant guests with memorable experience. One can choose a wide range of cities, hotels & resorts, packages and activities. Tour Packages are not only highly customizable but also best use of the money.
