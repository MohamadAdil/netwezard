Kaza

Pleasing Shimla Kaza 5 Nights and 6 Days


Destinations covered: (Shimla 02 nights) (Kaza 03 Nights)

Day 01: Dehli - Shimla (342Km)

Meal: Dinner

As you will arrive at Delhi Airport/ Railway station you will be met, assisted by our well-trained drive and proceed to Shimla. On arrival at Shimla check-in to your prebooked hotel and Dinner and night stay there.

Day 02: Shimla Kufri - Shimla Local

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, proceed for the local sightseeing of Shimla visiting Kufri, Kufri is a picturesque hill station situated 15 km from Shimla in Himachal Pradesh.  Evening free to stroll in the famous shopping place of Shimla town – 'THE MALL & THE RIDG, explore the oldest Christ church of Shimla, the Jakhoo temple, Scandal point and the famous lakkar bazaar in the evening back to the hotel for overnight stay.

Day 03: Shimla - Kaza via Kullu Valley (250Km)

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

This day after breakfast checkout from the hotel and proceed to Kaza, on the way visit Sunder Nagar Lake, Pandoh dam and Vaishno Devi temple. In the evening check-in to your hotel and dinner and overnight stay there.

Day 04: Kaza - Solang Valley - Kaza - Rohtang pass as per NGT

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

This day after breakfast proceed to Solang Valley the Snow point in Kaza. you can enjoy the various adventure sports activities over there. In the evening back to hotel and overnight stay there.

Day 05: Kaza - Local

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

This day after breakfast you will proceed for the local sightseeing of Kaza covering Vashisht, Hidimba Devi temple, Clubhouse, Tibetan monastery and van vihar in the evening back to hotel & overnight stay there.

Day 06: Kaza - Delhi Departure (539km)

Meal: Breakfast

After breakfast checkout from the hotel and get driven to Delhi airport/ railway station to catch your further flight/ train.
