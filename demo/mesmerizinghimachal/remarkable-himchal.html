Remarkable Taj With Himalayas

Remarkable Taj With Himalayas 08 Nights 09 Days


Thank you very much for your kind interest shown towards our travel organization & as per your requirement we are pleased to offer HIMACHAL tour package, details given below.

Destinations Covered: (Shimla 02 Nights) (Manali 03 Nights) (Chandigarh 01 Night) (Delhi 02 Nights)

Day 01: Delhi – Shimla (342Km)

Meal: Dinner

As you will arrive at Delhi Airport/ Railway station you will be met, assisted by our well-trained drive and proceed to Shimla. On arrival at Shimla check-in to your prebooked hotel and Dinner and night stay there.

Day 02: Shimla Kufri – Shimla Local

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, proceed for the local sightseeing of Shimla visiting Kufri, Kufri is a picturesque hill station situated 15 km from Shimla in Himachal Pradesh. Evening free to stroll in the famous shopping place of Shimla town – “THE MALL & THE RIDGE”, explore the oldest Christ church of Shimla, the Jakhoo temple, Scandal point and the famous lakkar bazaar in the evening back t the hotel for overnight stay.

Day 03: Shimla – Manali via Kullu Valley (250Km)

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

This day after breakfast checkout from the hotel and proceed to Manali, on the way visit Sunder Nagar Lake, pandoh dam and Vaishno Devi temple. In the evening checkin to your hotel and dinner and overnight stay there.

Day 04: Manali – Solang Valley – Manali – Rohtang pass as per NGT

Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

This day after breakfast proceed to Solang Valley the Snow point in Manali. you can enjoy the various adventure sport activities over there. In the evening back to hotel and overnight stay there.

Day 05: Manali – Local

Meal: Breakfast

This day after breakfast you will proceed for the local sightseeing of Manali covering Vashisht, Hidimba Devi temple, Club house, Tibetan monastery and van vihar in the evening back to hotel & overnight stay there.

Day 06: Manali – Chandigarh

Meal: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast checkout from the hotel and drive to Chandigarh, on arrival checkin to your hotel and overnight stay there.

Day 07: Chandigarh – Delhi (537 Kms/ 12-14 hours journey)

Meal: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast checkout from the hotel proceed for the local sightseeing of Chandigarh, visit Rose Garden, Rock Garden & Sukhna lake and get driven to Delhi, on arrival checkin your hotel and overnight stay at Delhi hotel.

Day 08: Delhi – Agra – Delhi (Agra closed on Friday)

Meal: Breakfast & Dinner

After your early breakfast proceed to Agra, visit the famous Taj Mahal, & Agra fort. In the evening back to Delhi hotel and overnight stay there.

Day 09: Delhi Local sightseeing – Delhi Departure (Delhi closed on Monday)

Meal: Breakfast

After breakfast checkout from the hotel and proceed for the local sightseeing of Delhi visiting the Red fort, India Gate, Qutab minar, Gandhi Smriti and in the evening drop back at Delhi airport/ railway station to catch your further flight/ train.
