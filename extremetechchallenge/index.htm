What is ExTec?

Technology has evolved significantly in recent years with the advent of mobile social products built on cloud infrastructure - significantly changing the opportunity for entrepreneurs to create new products and companies of great value with very little capital. Companies such as Facebook were created by a single student in a dorm room; products like Twitter were initially created and launched in 7 days.

The Extreme Tech Competition has been established to help identify the next generation of great entrepreneurs and to bring them resources in the form of prizes that help them:
1. bring their products to market quickly,

2. run their operations at low cost through donated services,
3. and to surround themselves with experienced mentors to help them turn their products into successful companies.

The competition is modeled after contests organized by venture capitalist Bill Tai and Red Bull Athlete / President of MaiTai Global, Susi Mai - collectively known as “MaiTai Global”. An unusual twist is that the judging panel is comprised of extreme sports practitioners in kiteboarding affiliated with MaiTai Global (http://bit.ly/MaiTai2013BloombergTV).
