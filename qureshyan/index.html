About Islam

Islam has roots in the faith of Abraham, who called people to worship One God, and is often referred to as one of the 'Abrhamic' (monotheistic) faiths, together with Judaism and Christianity. Like the Bible, the Qur'an mentions prophets, angels, miracles, good and bad deeds, reward and punishment, repentance and forgiveness, Heaven and Hell, Adam and Eve, the Torah and the Gospel and many other things that are familiar to Western tradition. The word 'Islam' comes from the Arabic word 'aslama', meaning 'peace' and 'submission'.
