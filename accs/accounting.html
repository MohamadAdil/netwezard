Accounting

Accountancy Solutions (Nottingham) Ltd is a UK based company that has been providing finance and accountancy services to small and medium sized businesses. About five years ago we set up our own processing company in Chandigarh, India which is managed by UK trained Chartered Accountants using state of the art technology and up to date UK software solutions.

After several years of dedication and hard work to bring our India operations to UK standards we are pleased to offer outsourcing facilities to other UK firms of Accountants, enabling them to achieve significant cost savings by outsourcing some of the basic accounting functions.
