BIG NEON
Launching a music-ticketing platform with one of crypto's biggest legends

The Situation

The founder of Monero, one of the world's most successful cryptocurrencies, and founder of Ticketfly, one of the world's most successful ticketing platforms, came to us with big plans to launch the first music-ticketing platform built on the blockchain. They had no brand, no product, and no in-house design team but they had a bold vision and we were amped to get involved.

The Solution

We started by creating a brand identity for Big neon that would capture its powerful combination of music and technology. We then worked with their internal PM and engineering teams to design and launch their mobile & web apps. In parallel, one of our founders served as an interim design executive helping them to recruit and build an internal world-class design team that they still rely on today.

How we helped

  • Interim Design Leadership
  • Brand Identity
  • UI / UX
  • Mobile App Design
  • Web App Design
  • Swag Design
  • Design Recruiting

After designing Big Neon's brand from scratch, we applied their new brand guidelines to design their mobile and web apps.

We developed a visual style which captured the emotional feeling of going to a music show while keeping things extremely clean and intuitive.

The logo that we developed for Big Neon was so simple yet unique that we leveraged it by itself to create a variety of swag for artists.

“Powerhouse was an invaluable partner. They designed our brand from scratch, were intrumental in the design of our product, and helped us recruit a world-class in-house design team.”

NAVEEN JAIN Co-founder, BigNeon
