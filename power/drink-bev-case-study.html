BEV
Designing the DTC home for

Bev's zero sugar canned wine

The Situation

When Bev approached us, their sugar-free canned wine was already a winning product. With their vibrant, outspoken personality, they were seeing good reach on social media, but they wanted us to bring that energy into their digital experience and make DTC a prominent part of their business.

The Solution

We helped them bring their brand to life with a full redesign and relaunch of DrinkBev.com. At every touch point, we implemented best-in-class CRO strategies, driving a lift in DTC performance across the board. Sixty days after launch, Bev was seeing a staggering 300% increase in sales MoM. Along with reinforcing key value props, we identified optimizations to increase conversion rate, average order value, retention, and ultimately lifetime value.

How we helped
