Welcome to Tyreplus –Edinburghs newest Tyre fitters for brand name and cheap tyres. With a huge range of tyres in stock we can find the tyres you need at the price you want to pay – including leading brands like Avon, Bridgestone, Continental, Dunlop and Falken tyres, plus Federal, Firestone, Fullrun, Goodyear, Kenda, Kumho, Maxxis, Michelin, Nankang and Pirelli tyres, as well as Uniroyal and Yokohama too.
