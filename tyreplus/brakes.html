Brake Pads

When you're looking for brake pads, reliability and high performance are the two things that you keep in mind. For this reason it is important to buy your brake pads from a well respected company. Here at EBC Brakes Direct, we can offer you the advice and the right products to perfectly suit you and your vehicle.

Car brake pads do not last forever and gradually they will wear out. This means that they will need to be replaced. Brake Pads should be inspected on a regular basis and they should be replaced as soon as they have worn down to their minimum. Here at Tyreplus, we can provide you with the relevant parts should your brake pads need changing.

Brake Discs

Although they don't need replacing as often as brake pads, it is important to replace your brake discs periodically. The Brake Discs slow or stop completely, the rotation of the wheel. The brake discs are connected to the wheel and it order to stop the wheel, the brake pads are forced against the disc when you apply pressure to the brake pedal.

Car brake discs need to be replaced every so often. It is a good idea to check them every time you check your brake pads. If you have noticed your car pulling to one side when you brake or even making a noise, then it may be time to replace your car discs Here at Tyreplus we can provide you with all the necessary parts for your vehicle.
